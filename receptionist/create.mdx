title: "Create a receptionist"
description: "Make a new receptionist, either from your business's website or by hand, and check what was filled in for you."

A receptionist is the voice that answers your business's phone. This page shows how to make a new one. If your business has a website, My Receptionist can read it and fill in much of the set-up for you. You then check what it filled in and add the rest.

Nothing you set up reaches a phone call until you press **Go live**.

## Before you start

- People with the **Owner** or **Staff** role can make a receptionist. Someone with the **Customer** role does not see the button. See [People and roles](/workspace/people-and-roles).
- Have your website address ready, for example `bayside-dental.com.au`. Only public pages are read.

## Start from your website

{/* SHOT: the Receptionists page with the New receptionist dialog open, showing the Business website box and the three buttons Cancel, Set it up by hand and Read the website */}

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the New receptionist box">
    On the **Receptionists** page, press **New receptionist** at the top. You can also press **Start from a website** under the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type your website address">
    In **Business website**, type the address of your business's website. You do not need to type `https://`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press Read the website">
    The button reads **Starting…** for a moment. Then your new receptionist's page opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait while it reads">
    The page shows **Reading your website…** with a line that changes as it works: **Finding your pages…**, then for example **Reading 7 pages…**, then **Working out the details…** and **Finishing…**. It takes up to a minute or two. You can leave the page. The reading carries on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check what was filled in">
    The receptionist's page opens with the cards filled in. A line at the top says how many things came from your website. Check each one, as described below.
  </Step>
</Steps>

{/* SHOT: a receptionist's page while the website is being read, showing the "Reading your website…" card and its progress line */}

If you do not see the **Business website** box, reading websites is not available in your workspace. The box goes straight to setting up by hand.

## What it fills in

My Receptionist can fill in these rows from your website:

| Card | Rows it can fill in |
|---|---|
| **Your business** | **Business name**, **About the business**, **What callers usually need help with**, **Advice the receptionist must never give**, **Timezone** |
| **Opening hours** | **Office hours** |
| **What it says** | **Words to listen for** |

**About the business** is filled in only if your receptionist has that row. When it fills in the business name, it also sets **Name in your list** to the business name followed by "Receptionist".

It never fills in the voice, the phone number, call recording, the greetings, or what happens to calls during office hours, outside them or on public holidays. It does not add any people or teams. You add those yourself on the **People and teams** card. See [People and teams](/receptionist/people-and-teams).

If something it found cannot be matched to what your website says, or is too long, it is left empty rather than guessed.

## Check what was filled in

Every row filled in from your website is marked so you can find it:

- The row is shaded, and has a small **From your website** tag beside its name. The tag is a link: press it to open, in a new tab, the page of your website the value came from.
- A line at the top of the page says, for example, "We filled in 6 things from your website. Check them." After **Still to check:** it names each row. Press a name to jump to that row.

{/* SHOT: a receptionist's page just after reading a website: the "We filled in 6 things from your website. Check them." line with "Still to check:" names and the "These look right" button, and below it a Your business card row with the "From your website" tag */}

To clear the marks:

- **Change the value.** Open the row, correct it, and its mark goes once the change is saved.
- **Press These look right** on a card, to accept every marked row on that card.
- **Press These look right** in the line at the top, to accept every marked row on the page at once.

<Warning>
Your receptionist cannot go live while any row is still marked. Until you have checked them all, the Go live bar lists, for example, "Check the 6 things we filled in from your website".
</Warning>

Read each value carefully before accepting it. For example, a dental practice's website might list Saturday hours that only apply in school holidays: correct **Office hours** before you accept it.

## Pages for the knowledge base

If your website has pages that would help the receptionist answer questions (such as prices, services or frequently asked questions), a box appears on the page headed **Add these pages to the knowledge base?** It lists the pages it found, all ticked.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Untick any page you do not want">
    The receptionist answers callers' questions from its knowledge base, so leave ticked only pages with correct, current information.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press Add to knowledge base">
    The button reads **Adding…** while it works. A message then says, for example, "3 pages added to the knowledge base. They are being read now."
  </Step>
</Steps>

This makes a new knowledge base, named after your business, and gives it to this receptionist. The pages are kept up to date: they are read again regularly, so changes to your website are picked up. Only people who can choose a knowledge base see this box.

Press **Skip** if you do not want any of them. If you press **Add to knowledge base** with nothing ticked, the box says "Tick at least one page, or choose Skip." See [What it knows](/receptionist/what-it-knows).

## Set it up by hand

Use this if your business has no website, or you would rather type everything yourself.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the New receptionist box">
    Press **New receptionist** on the **Receptionists** page. If the **Business website** box shows, press **Set it up by hand**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the boxes">
    - **Business name**: the name callers hear.
    - **Template**: the kind of receptionist it starts from, which sets how a call goes. Leave the first choice unless you were told to pick another.
    - **Name in your list**: what this receptionist is called in your list. It fills in by itself as you type the business name. Callers never hear it.
    - **Receptionist's name**: the name it gives callers. It starts as **Ava**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press Create">
    The receptionist's page opens, with the message "Receptionist created. Work through each part, then press Go live."
  </Step>
</Steps>

{/* SHOT: the New receptionist dialog after pressing "Set it up by hand", showing Business name, Template, Name in your list and Receptionist's name */}

If you already have a receptionist with the same business name, a **Short name** box appears with the message "There is already a receptionist with this short name. Change the short name, then press Create." The short name tells your receptionists apart. It can only use lowercase letters, digits and hyphens. Callers never see it.

## What can go wrong

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| "We could not read that website. You can set it up by hand." | Check the address. If it is right, the site may be blocking visitors or be down. Fill in the page yourself. |
| "That address sends visitors to bayside-dental.net.au. If that is your website, enter that address instead." (with the other site's name) | Your address forwards to a different website. Type the address it names, if that is yours. |
| "Reading your website did not finish. You can set it up by hand." | The reading stopped. Your receptionist is made, but empty. Fill it in yourself. |
| "We read your website but found nothing we could fill in. Set it up below by hand." | Nothing usable was found. Fill in the cards yourself. |
| "Too many websites read just now. Try again later, or set it up by hand." | There is a limit on how many websites can be read in an hour and in a day. Wait, or set it up by hand. |
| "We read the first 12 pages of your website." | Only the first 12 pages were read. Check that nothing important is missing. |
| "Your website has more text than we read; we read the first part of it." | Only the first part of your website was read. Check that nothing important is missing. |

If the reading fails, the receptionist is still made. You carry on from its page as if you had set it up by hand. Its business name will be your website's address, so change it on the **Your business** card first.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does anything from my website go to callers straight away?">
    No. Nothing reaches a phone call until you press **Go live**, and **Go live** is refused while any row is still marked **From your website**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why are no people or teams filled in?">
    The people and teams decide where real calls end up, so My Receptionist leaves them to you. Add them on the **People and teams** card.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What business name does it use while it reads?">
    Until the reading finishes, the business name is your website's address, for example "bayside-dental.com.au", and the receptionist's name is "Ava". Both can be changed on the **Your business** card.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I cannot see the New receptionist button.">
    Your role does not allow adding receptionists. Ask the workspace's owner. See [People and roles](/workspace/people-and-roles).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
