The **Your business** card holds what the receptionist knows before it answers a call: whose phone it is answering, what name it gives, what callers usually want, and what it must not advise on. This page explains each row on that card. The card's own line reads "What the receptionist knows before it answers."

{/* SHOT: a receptionist's page, the Your business card filled in for a plumbing business, showing all its rows */}

## The rows

| What you see | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Business name** | The name callers hear, for example "Thank you for calling Harbour Plumbing". |
| **Receptionist's name** | The name the receptionist gives for itself, for example "This is Ava". |
| **Name in your list** | What this receptionist is called in your list of receptionists. Callers never hear it. |
| **About the business** | A few sentences on what the business does, where, and who for. |
| **What callers usually need help with** | Everyday examples of why people ring. |
| **Advice the receptionist must never give** | The kind of advice it must always pass to your team. |
| **Timezone** | Your business's local time. |

A row with nothing in it shows **Not added yet** (or **Not chosen yet** for the timezone).

If you do not see **About the business**, your receptionist does not use it.

## Change a row

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the row">
    Click the row on the **Your business** card. A panel opens on the right with one box.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type in the box">
    The help under the box says what to write. Your change saves by itself about two seconds after you stop typing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press Done">
    The panel closes and the row shows the new value.
  </Step>
</Steps>

{/* SHOT: the edit panel for Business name, showing the box and the help line under it */}

Callers hear your changes only after you press **Go live** (or **Publish changes**). See [Go live](/receptionist/go-live).

## Each box in detail

### Business name

The name callers hear. The standard greetings say it, for example "Thank you for calling Harbour Plumbing". Write it the way you say it on the phone.

If it is empty, the receptionist cannot go live, and the page says "Enter the business name."

### Receptionist's name

The name the receptionist gives for itself, such as "This is Ava". The standard greeting for when the office is open says it. A new receptionist starts as **Ava**.

If it is empty, the page says "Enter the receptionist's name." and the receptionist cannot go live.

### Name in your list

What this receptionist is called on your **Receptionists** page. Callers never hear it. It is useful when you have more than one, for example "Harbour Plumbing After Hours".

It cannot be empty: the page says "Enter a name for your list."

### About the business

Optional. Two or three sentences on what the business does, where, and who for. The receptionist always has this in mind, so it understands what callers are talking about.

For example: "Bayside Vets is a small-animal vet clinic in Manly. We see dogs, cats and rabbits, and offer vaccinations, desexing and dental care."

Keep it short. Prices, policies and other detail belong in the knowledge base. See [What it knows](/receptionist/what-it-knows).

The limit is 600 characters. Over that, the row shows, for example, "About the business is 720 characters long. Keep it to 600 or fewer, and put the detail in the knowledge base." and the receptionist cannot go live.

### What callers usually need help with

Optional. A few examples in everyday words, separated by commas. For a law firm: "buying or selling a house, a will, a dispute, a contract".

When a caller describes one of these, the receptionist puts them through to the right person instead of reading out a list of services.

### Advice the receptionist must never give

For example "legal advice" for a law firm, "medical advice" for a dental practice, or "repair or safety advice" for a trade. If a caller asks for it, the receptionist says that needs to go to the team and offers to put them through.

A new receptionist starts with "professional advice" here. Change it to suit your business. This box must be filled in: if it is empty, the page says "Say what advice the receptionist must never give." and the receptionist cannot go live.

### Timezone

Your business's local time. Your office hours, and each person's hours on the **People and teams** card, are in this time. A new receptionist starts on **Australia/Sydney**. To change it, choose from the list, for example **Australia/Perth**. If none is chosen yet, the list starts with **Choose…**.

If no timezone is chosen, the page says "Choose a timezone." and the receptionist cannot go live.

## Filled in from your website

If you made the receptionist from your website, some rows on this card may carry a **From your website** tag. Check each one, change it if needed, then press **These look right** on the card. See [Create a receptionist](/receptionist/create#check-what-was-filled-in).

## When the card is locked

If you see "Your provider has locked this." beside the card's title, the rows cannot be opened. Ask whoever looks after your receptionist (someone with the **Owner** or **Staff** role) to make the change. See [Access and locks](/workspace/access-and-locks).

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does changing the business name change the greetings?">
    The standard greetings change by themselves. A greeting you wrote yourself changes only if you used the short code for the business name in it. See [What it says](/receptionist/what-it-says).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Should I put my prices in About the business?">
    No. Keep that box to two or three sentences. Put prices, policies and other detail in the knowledge base, where the receptionist looks up answers to questions.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does a problem about the timezone show on the Business name row?">
    Problems with the names, the timezone and the advice box all show on the **Business name** row. Open the row the message names to fix it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
