title: "Confirm it is you"
description: "Why some changes to your account ask for your password again, and how to get past the Confirm it is you page."
---

Some changes to how you sign in are important enough that My Receptionist checks it is really you first, even though you are already signed in. This stops someone who finds your computer signed in from taking over your account, and with it your receptionist's settings.

When you make one of these changes, the page **Confirm it is you** opens. You confirm with your password, or by signing in again with Google or Microsoft. After that you are not asked again for 15 minutes.

{/* SHOT: the "Confirm it is you" page with the "Your password" box, the Confirm button, "or", and a "Continue with Google" button */}

## When you are asked

You are asked before you:

- connect or disconnect a Google or Microsoft account (see [Your password and sign-in methods](/account/password#google-and-microsoft));
- switch two-step sign-in on, finish setting it up, or switch it off (see [Two-step sign-in](/account/two-step-sign-in));
- make new recovery codes;
- use **Sign out everywhere**.

Changing your password does not use this page. It asks for your current password on the same form instead.

## Confirm with your password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Type your password">
    In **Your password**, type the password you sign in with.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Confirm">
    You go back to the page you were on. It says: "Thank you. Now do it again: you will not be asked to confirm for the next 15 minutes."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the button again">
    The first click only took you to **Confirm it is you**. It did not make the change. Click the same button again to make it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Confirm with Google or Microsoft

If a Google or Microsoft account is connected to you, the page also shows **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Microsoft**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Continue with Google or Continue with Microsoft">
    You go to Google's or Microsoft's sign-in page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in there again">
    Use the same account you connected. You may need to type your Google or Microsoft password again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the button again">
    You come back to the page you were on, with the same "Thank you. Now do it again" message. Click the button again to make the change.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Google often does not ask you to sign in again, and then it cannot confirm it is you. If that happens, confirm with your password instead.
</Note>

To leave without confirming, click **Cancel**.

## What can go wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| "That is not your password." | Type the password you sign in with. Check that Caps Lock is off. |
| "Too many wrong passwords. Sign in again." | 5 wrong passwords in a row sign you out. Sign in again from the sign-in page. |
| "Too many sign-in attempts. Wait a while and try again, or use “Forgot your password?”." | There have been too many wrong passwords for your address. Wait a while, or reset your password. |
| A message saying Google or Microsoft "did not show that you signed in again just now, so it cannot confirm it is you" | Confirm with your password instead. |
| A message saying the Google or Microsoft account "was connected less than 15 minutes ago" | An account you have only just connected cannot confirm it is you yet. Use your password, or try again later. |
| "That Google account is not the one connected to you. Use the one you connected, or your password." | You signed in to a different Google account. The same applies to Microsoft. |
| "Signing in with Google did not work. Try again." | You cancelled, or something went wrong on the way. Try again. |

## If you have no password of your own

If you accepted your invitation with Google or Microsoft and never chose a password, the page shows only the Google or Microsoft button, and says: "You can also choose a password: sign out and use “Forgot your password?” on the sign-in page."

If you have no password and no Google or Microsoft account that can be used, the page says: "You have not chosen a password, and no Google or Microsoft account that can be used here is connected. To choose a password, sign out and use “Forgot your password?” on the sign-in page." Do that, then try the change again. See [Sign in](/get-started/sign-in#forgot-your-password).

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why do I have to confirm when I am already signed in?">
    So that someone who sits down at your computer while you are signed in cannot change how your account signs in, or lock you out.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How long does confirming last?">
    15 minutes. Within that time you can make several of these changes without being asked again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I confirmed but nothing changed.">
    Confirming only lets you through. Click the button for the change again, as the "Now do it again" message says.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
