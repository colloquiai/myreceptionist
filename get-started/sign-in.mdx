You sign in to change how your receptionist answers calls, see your call history, and manage your workspace.

{/* SHOT: the sign-in page, with the Email address and Password boxes, "Forgot your password?", the "Keep me signed in on this computer" tick box and the Sign in button */}

## Sign in with your email and password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Type your email address">
    In **Email address**, type the address you signed up with, or the one you were invited with.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type your password">
    In **Password**, type your password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose whether to stay signed in">
    Tick **Keep me signed in on this computer** if this is your own computer. See [Staying signed in](#staying-signed-in) below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Sign in">
    If you belong to one workspace, it opens. If you belong to more than one, you choose which one to open. See [Find your way around](/get-started/find-your-way#choosing-a-workspace).
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you have switched on two-step sign-in, you are then asked for a code from your phone. See [Two-step sign-in](/account/two-step-sign-in#signing-in-with-two-step-sign-in).

## Sign in with Google or Microsoft

Some sign-in pages also show **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Microsoft** above the email and password boxes, with "or" between them. If yours shows neither button, sign in with your email and password.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Continue with Google or Continue with Microsoft">
    You go to Google's or Microsoft's own sign-in page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in there">
    Use the Google or Microsoft account for the same email address that you use for My Receptionist.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Come back signed in">
    You return to My Receptionist, signed in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Google or Microsoft can only sign you in to an account that already exists. It does not make a new account. The first time, it works when Google or Microsoft confirms that you own the email address of your My Receptionist account. You can also connect Google or Microsoft to your account yourself. See [Your password and sign-in methods](/account/password#google-and-microsoft).

| What you see | What it means |
|---|---|
| "There is no account for (your address) yet. Ask whoever manages your receptionist to add you." | Nobody has added this email address. Ask the owner of your workspace to add you. |
| "To use Google with this account, sign in with your password first and connect Google on your account page." | Google could not be matched to your account by email address alone. Sign in with your password, then connect Google on your account page. |
| "Signing in with Google did not work. Try again, or sign in with your password." | You cancelled, or something went wrong on the way. Try again. The same message is shown for Microsoft. |
| A message saying Google or Microsoft "did not confirm that you own" your address | Sign in with your password, or ask the person who looks after your company's Google or Microsoft accounts. |

## Staying signed in

- If you do not tick **Keep me signed in on this computer**, you are signed out after two hours without using the page.
- If you tick it, you stay signed in on that computer for up to 24 hours.
- Do not tick it on a shared or public computer.

**Keep me signed in on this computer** works with email and password sign-in only. Signing in with Google or Microsoft does not offer it.

Changing your password, or using **Sign out everywhere** on your account page, signs you out on every other computer, even where you ticked the box. See [Your password](/account/password).

## Forgot your password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Forgot your password?">
    It is above the **Password** box on the sign-in page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type your email address">
    On the **Forgot your password?** page, type your email address and click **Email me a link**. The page says: "If that address has an account, a link to reset the password has been sent to it."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the email and click its link">
    The link works for 60 minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a new password">
    On **Choose a new password**, type a new password of at least 12 characters in **New password** and again in **New password again**. Click **Save the new password**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    You go back to the sign-in page. Sign in with your new password.
  </Step>
</Steps>

{/* SHOT: the "Forgot your password?" page after clicking "Email me a link", showing the green message */}

Resetting your password signs you out everywhere else, and you get an email saying **Your password was reset**. If a Google or Microsoft account is connected to you, the sign-in page also says "These can sign in as you:" and names them. If you do not recognise one, sign in and click **Disconnect** beside it on your account page.

<Note>
At most 3 reset emails a day are sent to one address. If you asked several times and nothing arrived, check your junk or spam folder, or wait until tomorrow.
</Note>

If the page says "This link to reset the password is not valid or has expired. Ask for a new one.", start again from **Forgot your password?**.

## Accepting an invitation

When the owner of a workspace adds you, you get an email with the subject **You have been added to** and the workspace's name. Its link works once, for 7 days.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Choose your password in the email">
    The page **Choose your password** opens with your email address filled in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a password">
    Type a password of at least 12 characters in **Password** and again in **Password again**. Click **Save the password and sign in**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Or use Google or Microsoft">
    If the page shows **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Microsoft**, you can use that account instead. It must be the account for the address the invitation was sent to.
  </Step>
</Steps>

{/* SHOT: the "Choose your password" page opened from an invitation, showing "You have been invited." */}

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| "This link no longer works" | The link was used or more than 7 days have passed. Ask the person who invited you to send it again. |
| "Sign out first" | Someone else is signed in on this browser. Click **Sign out**, then open the link in the email again. |
| "The two passwords are not the same." | Type the same password in both boxes. |
| "The password field must be at least 12 characters." | Choose a longer password. |
| "This invitation is for (address). You signed in to Google as (another address)." | Use the Google or Microsoft account for the invited address, or choose a password. |

If you already have a password, for example because you belong to another workspace, you are not asked to choose one. The email says **Sign in with your usual email address and password**, with a button to open the workspace.

## What can go wrong when signing in

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| "These credentials do not match our records." | The email address or password is wrong. Check both, or use **Forgot your password?**. |
| "Too many sign-in attempts. Wait a while and try again, or use “Forgot your password?”." | There were too many wrong tries. Wait a while before trying again, or reset your password. |
| "No access" | You signed in, but your address is not part of any workspace. Ask whoever gave you the address to add you. |
| "Page expired" | The page was open too long. Go back, reload the page and sign in again. |

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I sign in on my phone?">
    Yes. The pages work in a phone's web browser.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I accepted my invitation with Google and never chose a password. How do I get one?">
    Sign out, then use **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page with your email address. The link lets you choose a password.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who can add me to a workspace?">
    The workspace's owner, on the **People** page. See [People and roles](/workspace/people-and-roles).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
