Your receptionist can record its calls so you can listen back later. Recording is on unless you turn it off. This page explains the choice, what callers are told, and where recordings are kept.

## The choice

The **Call recording** row is on the [Voice and number](/receptionist/voice-and-number) card. It has two choices:

| Choice | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Record calls** | The receptionist's calls are recorded. This is the standard choice. |
| **Do not record calls** | The receptionist's calls are not recorded. |

Choose **Do not record calls** if the business does not want its callers recorded.

Like your greetings and hours, a change here only affects calls once you go live or publish your changes. See [Go live](/receptionist/go-live).

## What callers are told

Nothing, with the standard wording. None of the standard greetings mention recording, and the receptionist is not told to say anything about it.

If you want callers to hear something about recording, write your own greetings on the [What it says](/receptionist/what-it-says) card. There are three greetings to think about: **Greeting when the office is open**, **Greeting when the office is closed** and **Greeting on a public holiday**.

<Note>
If you put every call straight through during office hours, the open greeting is the only thing the receptionist says before connecting the caller.
</Note>

## Listening to a recording

Recordings are on the [Calls](/calls/call-history) page.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Calls page">
    Click **Calls** in the side menu, then choose the receptionist.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the call">
    Click the call in the list to open it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Play or download it">
    Click **Play recording** to listen, or **Download** to save a copy.
  </Step>
</Steps>

When someone plays or downloads a recording, the [Activity](/workspace/activity) page notes it, with who did it.

## When there is no recording

Instead of the player, the call shows **No recording for this call.** with one of these reasons:

| What it says | What it means |
|---|---|
| This call was not recorded. | The call was not recorded, for example because recording was off. |
| The recording is not ready yet. Try again in a few minutes. | The recording is still being prepared, usually because the call has only just finished. |
| The recording has been deleted. | The recording is no longer kept. |
| The recording is not available. | There is no recording for another reason. |

If you leave a recording open for a while, it may stop playing and the page says "The link to the recording ran out. Press Play recording again." Press **Play recording** and it starts again.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I record some calls and not others?">
    No. The choice covers every call the receptionist answers. If you have more than one receptionist, each has its own **Call recording** row.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does turning recording off change anything else on the call?">
    No. The receptionist greets, puts calls through and takes messages in the same way.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who can listen to recordings?">
    Anyone who can sign in to your workspace and see the receptionist can open its calls and play the recordings. See [People and roles](/workspace/people-and-roles).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
