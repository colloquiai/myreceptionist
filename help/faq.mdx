Short answers to the questions people ask most, grouped by subject. Each answer links to the page with the detail. If something is not working, see [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting).

## Getting started

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What does My Receptionist do?">
    It answers your business's phone. It greets callers with your business's name, puts them through to the right person or team during office hours, takes messages, and answers simple questions from your own website and files. See [Welcome to My Receptionist](/).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What do I need before I start?">
    An email address, your opening hours, and the names, phone numbers and email addresses of the people callers ask for. A website helps, because My Receptionist can read it and fill in much of the set-up. A payment method is needed before the receptionist can go live. See [Quickstart](/get-started/quickstart).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I try it before I pay?">
    A receptionist that is not on a plan yet may have free test minutes, so it can answer real calls before you choose a plan. A payment method is still needed to go live. When the test minutes run out it stops answering until it is on a plan. See [Test minutes](/billing/test-minutes).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I have to finish the set-up in one go?">
    No. Changes save by themselves, so you can stop and come back. Nothing reaches callers until you press **Go live**. See [The receptionist page](/receptionist/the-receptionist-page).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I keep my existing business number?">
    The receptionist answers on a number chosen from the **Phone number** list on the **Voice and number** card. That list shows the numbers in your workspace. If the number you want is not there, contact support. See [Voice and number](/receptionist/voice-and-number).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I have more than one receptionist?">
    Yes. A workspace can hold several, for example one for each office. Each has its own number, hours, people and wording, and each needs its own plan. See [Make a receptionist](/receptionist/create).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use it on my phone?">
    Yes. The pages work in a phone's web browser. The side menu becomes a bar across the top, and the edit panel fills the screen. See [Find your way around](/get-started/find-your-way).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Calls

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What does a caller hear first?">
    One of three greetings, picked by the time in your timezone: the open greeting during office hours, the closed greeting outside them, or the public holiday greeting on a date in your list. With the standard wording the open greeting is, for example, "Thank you for calling Bayside Dental. This is Ava. How can I help you today?" See [How a call goes](/on-a-call/how-a-call-goes).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does it put calls through after hours?">
    No. Outside office hours and on public holidays it never puts calls through. It offers to take a message, or ends the call after the greeting if you chose that. See [Closed and public holidays](/on-a-call/closed-and-public-holidays).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if the person does not answer?">
    The caller comes back to the receptionist and hears "I wasn't able to put you through. Would you like to leave a message, or try someone else?" They can leave a message, ask for someone else, or finish the call. See [Putting calls through](/on-a-call/putting-calls-through).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where do messages go?">
    To the person the receptionist last tried to put the caller through to, by text or email as set on that person. Otherwise, by email to your main contact. Emailed messages to a person are copied to the main contact too. See [Taking messages](/on-a-call/taking-messages).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can it book appointments?">
    No. It puts calls through, takes messages and answers questions. It has no way to book, change or cancel appointments. A caller who wants to book can be put through to the right person, or leave a message.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does it know about public holidays?">
    Only the dates you add under **Public holidays** on the **Opening hours** card. Add each date for every year. See [Opening hours](/receptionist/opening-hours).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will it tell callers it is not a person?">
    If a caller sincerely asks, it says it is a virtual assistant and keeps it brief. See [How a call goes](/on-a-call/how-a-call-goes).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will it give out my staff's phone numbers?">
    No. It never reads out a phone number from your people and teams or your knowledge base. If a caller asks for a direct number, it offers to put them through instead. It also never confirms or denies whether someone is your client. See [Answering questions](/on-a-call/answering-questions).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are calls recorded?">
    Yes, unless you choose **Do not record calls** on the **Voice and number** card. The standard greetings do not mention recording. If you want callers told, write it into your own greetings. See [Call recording](/on-a-call/call-recording).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where can I see who rang and what was said?">
    On the **Calls** page, where call history is set up for your workspace. Open a call to read the conversation, see what the receptionist did and play the recording. See [Call history](/calls/call-history).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Setting up

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do changes take effect straight away?">
    No. Changes are saved as you make them, but callers keep hearing the version that last went live. Press **Publish changes** to send your changes to callers. The Go live bar says "You have changes that are not live yet." until you do. See [Go live](/receptionist/go-live).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens to calls while I am making changes?">
    Nothing changes for callers. They hear the version that last went live while you work. You can change several things over several days, then publish them all at once.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can more than one person look after a receptionist?">
    Yes. The workspace's owner adds people on the **People** tab of **Settings**. If two people change the same receptionist at once, the page holds back the second save and asks whether to load the other person's version or keep yours. See [People and roles](/workspace/people-and-roles) and [The receptionist page](/receptionist/the-receptionist-page#when-two-people-change-the-same-receptionist).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I have to write the greetings myself?">
    No. Every greeting and the goodbye have standard wording that uses your business name, the receptionist's name and your hours. Write your own only where you want something different. See [What it says](/receptionist/what-it-says).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What is the main contact for?">
    It takes calls that are not clearly for anyone, and messages for nobody in particular. Every receptionist needs exactly one, with an email address. It is often a shared line such as Reception. See [People and teams](/receptionist/people-and-teams#the-main-contact).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I stop it putting calls through at lunch?">
    Yes. Add two lines of office hours for the same days, for example 08:30 to 12:30 and 13:30 to 17:00. Between them, callers hear the closed greeting. See [Opening hours](/receptionist/opening-hours).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I add all my staff at once?">
    Yes, from a spreadsheet saved as a CSV file, up to 200 people in one file. Use **Import from a CSV file** on the **People and teams** page. See [Import people from a file](/receptionist/import-people).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the receptionist need a knowledge base?">
    No. Without one it still puts calls through and takes messages, but it cannot answer general questions. See [What it knows](/receptionist/what-it-knows).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if I delete a receptionist?">
    It stops answering calls, and this cannot be undone. Its phone number stays in your workspace, marked as having been on a deleted receptionist, so you can choose it for another one. Only the owner and staff can delete, and staff need the **May publish and delete** tick for one that has gone live. See [Find your way around](/get-started/find-your-way).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Billing

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where do I see prices?">
    On the **Billing** page. Prices and plans can change, so this help site does not list them. See [Billing](/billing/overview).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does each receptionist need its own plan?">
    Yes. Each price on the Billing page is for one receptionist, each month. A receptionist can be put on a plan once it has gone live. See [Plans and minutes](/billing/plans-and-minutes).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens when the minutes run out?">
    On a plan, it keeps answering calls, and each extra minute is charged at the price on the plan's card. On test minutes, it stops answering until you choose a plan. The Billing page shows which. See [Plans and minutes](/billing/plans-and-minutes) and [Test minutes](/billing/test-minutes).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I change my card or cancel a plan?">
    Press **Manage billing** on the Billing page. A separate payments page opens where you change your card, billing email or address, or cancel. See [Billing](/billing/overview).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who can see Billing?">
    Only an Owner of the workspace. If your receptionist stops answering and you are not the owner, ask them to look at the Billing page. See [People and roles](/workspace/people-and-roles).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## People and access

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is the difference between People and People and teams?">
    **People and teams**, on a receptionist's page, is who callers can be put through to. **People**, a tab of **Settings**, is who can sign in to your workspace. A receptionist at the front desk can be in both, but they are two separate lists. See [People and teams](/receptionist/people-and-teams) and [People and roles](/workspace/people-and-roles).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What are the roles?">
    **Owner** can do everything, including billing and who can sign in. **Staff** can work on every receptionist but not manage people or billing. **Customer** sees only the receptionists ticked for them. See [People and roles](/workspace/people-and-roles).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can someone change the receptionist without being able to make changes live?">
    Yes. Leave **May publish and delete** unticked. Their changes are saved, and someone who is allowed presses **Publish changes**. See [People and roles](/workspace/people-and-roles).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I look after receptionists for other businesses. Can each see only its own?">
    Yes. Give each business a login with the **Customer** role and tick its receptionist. You can also lock the parts of the receptionist they must not change. See [Access and locks](/workspace/access-and-locks).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use one sign-in for several businesses?">
    Yes. One email address can belong to several workspaces, with a different role in each. See [Several workspaces](/workspace/several-workspaces).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Security

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I protect my account?">
    Choose a password of at least 12 characters and switch on two-step sign-in, so signing in also needs a code from your phone. Keep your recovery codes somewhere safe. See [Two-step sign-in](/account/two-step-sign-in).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does it ask for my password when I am already signed in?">
    Changes to how you sign in, such as switching off two-step sign-in, need you to confirm it is you first. After that you are not asked again for 15 minutes. See [Confirm it is you](/account/confirm-it-is-you).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is my card kept by My Receptionist?">
    No. Your card, billing email and address are kept by the payments provider, on its own payments page. See [Billing](/billing/overview).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who can listen to call recordings?">
    Anyone who can sign in to your workspace and see that receptionist. See [Call recording](/on-a-call/call-recording).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Someone has left the business. What do I do?">
    Remove them on the **People** tab of **Settings** so they can no longer open the workspace. If they knew your own password, change it: that signs you out everywhere else too. Remove them from **People and teams** as well if callers were put through to them, then press **Publish changes**. See [People and roles](/workspace/people-and-roles).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I signed in on a computer I no longer trust.">
    Use **Sign out everywhere** on your account page, then change your password. See [Your password and sign-in methods](/account/password#signing-out-everywhere).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
