This page starts from what you notice, such as callers not being put through or a message that never arrived. Under each problem are the likely causes, most likely first. For each one you see where to look and what to do.

Two checks solve many problems, so try them first:

- **Is the receptionist live, with your latest changes?** Open the receptionist's page. The tag beside its name should say **Live**, and the Go live bar should say "Live. Callers hear the latest version." If the bar says "You have changes that are not live yet.", callers are still hearing the older version. See [Go live](/receptionist/go-live).
- **Is the timezone right?** On the **Your business** card, check **Timezone**. Office hours, public holidays and each person's hours all go by it. See [Your business](/receptionist/your-business).

## My receptionist is not answering

**1. It has not gone live.** Nothing you set up reaches a phone call until someone presses **Go live**.

- Check: on the **Receptionists** page, look at the tag on its row. **Not live** means it has never gone live. **Not live yet** means going live did not finish.
- Fix: open the receptionist and press **Go live**. See [Go live](/receptionist/go-live).

**2. It has no phone number connected.** A number is connected when the receptionist goes live, not when you choose it.

- Check: on the **Receptionists** page, the line under its name shows the number callers ring. If it says "No phone number yet", none is connected. Also check that this is the number you are ringing.
- Fix: on the **Voice and number** card, choose a number under **Phone number**, then press **Go live** or **Publish changes**. See [Voice and number](/receptionist/voice-and-number).

**3. Its test minutes have run out.** A receptionist that is not on a plan stops answering when its test minutes are used up. Only the **Billing** page shows this. The receptionist's own page and the list of receptionists do not.

- Check: the workspace's owner opens **Billing**. Under **Your receptionists**, look for a red **Stopped taking calls** tag beside it.
- Fix: choose a plan for it. You do not need to go live again. See [Test minutes](/billing/test-minutes).

**4. It was deleted somewhere else.** A **Deleted** tag means it no longer answers calls.

- Check: the tag beside its name says **Deleted**. On the list of receptionists, deleted ones are hidden: click **show** under the list to see them.
- Fix: open it and press **Go live** to make it again. See [Go live](/receptionist/go-live).

**5. It answers, then hangs up straight away.** This is not a fault if the call came in while you were closed and you chose **Say the greeting, then end the call**.

- Check: on the **Opening hours** card, look at **Outside office hours** and **On a public holiday**.
- Fix: choose **Offer to take a message** if you want callers to be able to leave one. See [Opening hours](/receptionist/opening-hours).

## Callers are not being put through

**1. The office was closed.** The receptionist puts calls through only during office hours. Outside them, and on public holidays, a caller who asks to be put through hears "I'm sorry, I can't put you through while the office is closed. Would you like to leave a message?"

- Check: the **Office hours** and **Public holidays** rows on the **Opening hours** card, and the **Timezone** on the **Your business** card.
- Fix: see [The receptionist said the office was closed when it was open](#the-receptionist-said-the-office-was-closed-when-it-was-open) below.

**2. The person's own hours had ended.** Each person has **Available** times. Outside them, calls are not put through to that person, even when the office is open.

- Check: on the **People and teams** card, open the person and look at **Available**. The short line under their name also shows their hours, for example "09:00 to 17:00".
- Fix: widen the times, or clear both times so they take calls at any time. See [People and teams](/receptionist/people-and-teams).

**3. The call rang the wrong phone, or nobody answered.** If a call is put through but not taken, the caller hears "I wasn't able to put you through." and is offered a message or someone else.

- Check: open the call on the **Calls** page. Under **What the receptionist did**, "Could not put the call through to" and a name means the call was tried but not taken. Then open the person on the **People and teams** card and check **Calls ring their** (**Desk number** or **Mobile**) and that the number is right.
- Fix: correct the number or the choice, then press **Publish changes**. See [Call history](/calls/call-history) and [Putting calls through](/on-a-call/putting-calls-through).

**4. The receptionist could not tell who the caller wanted.** When a caller asks for someone who is not listed, or it is not clear who they mean, the call goes to the main contact.

- Check: is the person the caller asked for on the **People and teams** card, under the name callers use? Is a team's **What it covers** clear?
- Fix: add the person, or add a team for that kind of call. Add names that are easy to mishear to **Words to listen for** on the **What it says** card. See [What it says](/receptionist/what-it-says).

**5. Every call goes to one person.** If **During office hours** is set to **Put every call straight through**, every call during office hours goes to the one person chosen, with nothing asked. If that person does not take it, or it is outside their **Available** times, the caller is offered a message and nobody else is tried.

- Check: the **During office hours** row on the **Opening hours** card. An amber warning on that row means the person's hours do not cover all your office hours.
- Fix: widen the person's **Available** times, choose someone else, or choose **The receptionist answers the call**. See [Opening hours](/receptionist/opening-hours).

**6. The change is not live yet.** A person you added, or a number you corrected, is not used on calls until you press **Publish changes**. See [My changes are not being heard on calls](#my-changes-are-not-being-heard-on-calls).

## The receptionist said the office was closed when it was open

At the start of every call the receptionist checks the day, date and time in your timezone. It then picks the greeting: public holiday, closed or open. See [Closed and public holidays](/on-a-call/closed-and-public-holidays).

**1. The timezone is wrong.** If it is set to another city, your hours are counted in that city's time.

- Check: **Timezone** on the **Your business** card. A new receptionist starts on **Australia/Sydney**, which is wrong for a business in Perth or Adelaide, for example.
- Fix: choose your own timezone, then press **Publish changes**. See [Your business](/receptionist/your-business).

**2. The office hours are not what you meant.** A day may not be ticked, or a time may be wrong, for example a closing time of 15:00 instead of 17:00.

- Check: open **Office hours** on the **Opening hours** card. Read **How the receptionist says it**: it is written from your hours, so it shows what the receptionist believes. Check every line, including any second line for a lunch break or a Saturday.
- Fix: correct the days and times, then press **Publish changes**. See [Opening hours](/receptionist/opening-hours).

**3. Today is in the public holidays list.** Only the exact dates in the list count. A date added by mistake, or for the wrong year, makes the receptionist say you are closed for the holiday.

- Check: the **Public holidays** row on the **Opening hours** card.
- Fix: remove the date with its ×, then press **Publish changes**.

**4. The call came in just before or after a change of hours.** The receptionist checks the time once, when it answers. A call answered at 4:59 pm is treated as open for the rest of the call.

**5. The hours were changed but not published.** Callers hear the hours that last went live. See [My changes are not being heard on calls](#my-changes-are-not-being-heard-on-calls).

The same causes apply the other way round, when it said you were open while you were closed.

## A message did not arrive

First open the call on the **Calls** page. **What happened** and **What the receptionist did** tell you whether a message was taken and where it went, for example "Message sent to Dr Patel by email" or "Message sent by text". See [Call history](/calls/call-history).

**1. No message was taken.** The receptionist does not take a message without a number to call back on. A caller who hung up, or who would not give a number, leaves no message.

- Check: on the call, **What happened** does not say "Message taken", and the **Conversation** shows how the call ended.
- Fix: nothing to fix. With **Say the greeting, then end the call** chosen for closed times, callers cannot leave a message at all. See [Taking messages](/on-a-call/taking-messages).

**2. It went to the main contact.** A message goes to the person the receptionist last tried to put the caller through to. If it tried nobody, for example because the office was closed or the caller asked straight away to leave a message, the message is emailed to the main contact, with the name of the person the caller asked for in it.

- Check: who is tagged **Main contact** on the **People and teams** card, and look in their email.
- Fix: nothing is wrong. If the wrong person is the main contact, open the right person and tick **Main contact**.

**3. It went by text, not email, or the other way round.** Each person's **Send their messages by** decides. With **Main contact only**, their messages go to the main contact and not to them.

- Check: open the person on the **People and teams** card and look at **Send their messages by**.
- Fix: choose **Text to their mobile** or **Email**, then press **Publish changes**.

**4. The email address or mobile number is wrong.** A typing mistake that still looks like a real address or number sends the message to the wrong place.

- Check: the person's **Email** and **Mobile** on the **People and teams** card.
- Fix: correct them, then press **Publish changes**.

**5. A text was expected at the main contact.** Messages sent to a person by text are not copied to the main contact. Only emailed messages are.

**6. The message could not be sent.** If **What the receptionist did** says "The message could not be sent by email" or "The message could not be sent by text", the caller was told "I'm sorry, I'm having trouble getting your message through right now. Please call back during business hours." The message was not delivered. Read the caller's details in the **Conversation** and contact support.

## It does not know the answer to a question

When it does not know, the receptionist says "I don't have that information. Would you like to leave a message, or be put through to someone who can help?" It is told not to guess. See [Answering questions](/on-a-call/answering-questions).

**1. No knowledge base is chosen.** Without one, the receptionist cannot answer general questions.

- Check: the **Knowledge base** row on the **What it knows** card. "None chosen" means there is none.
- Fix: choose or make one, then press **Publish changes**. See [What it knows](/receptionist/what-it-knows).

**2. The pages or files are not ready.** New items have to be read first.

- Check: open **What is in it** on the **What it knows** card. Each item should say **Ready**. **Waiting to be read** and **Being read** mean wait. **Failed** means it could not be read.
- Fix: wait, or remove a failed item with its ✕ and add it again.

**3. The answer is not in what it was given.** It answers only from its knowledge base.

- Check: is the answer on one of the pages or in one of the files listed under **What is in it**? A page you unticked when adding a website is not there. A website added with "read once" does not pick up later changes.
- Fix: add the page or a file with the answer. See [What it knows](/receptionist/what-it-knows).

**4. It is a kind of advice it must not give.** If the question is covered by **Advice the receptionist must never give** on the **Your business** card, it says that needs to go to the team and offers to put the caller through. It also never guesses at prices or timeframes unless your knowledge base states them, and never reads out a phone number.

**5. The caller stated a need, not a question.** "I'm selling my house" is put through to the right person, not answered. This is by design. See [Answering questions](/on-a-call/answering-questions#a-question-or-a-need).

## My changes are not being heard on calls

**1. They have not been published.** Changes save by themselves, but callers hear them only after you press **Go live** or **Publish changes**.

- Check: the Go live bar on the receptionist's page. "You have changes that are not live yet." means callers still hear the old version.
- Fix: press **Publish changes** and confirm. When it is done the page says "Live. Callers now hear this version." See [Go live](/receptionist/go-live).

**2. Going live did not finish.** If it stopped part way, the receptionist may show **Not live yet**, or the message box says "This did not finish. Press Go live again to carry on from where it stopped."

- Fix: press **Go live** again.

**3. You are not allowed to make changes live.** Your changes are saved, but someone else has to publish them. The bar says "Your changes are saved. Someone who is allowed to go live needs to press Go live." or you are told "Your changes are saved, but your account is not allowed to make them live."

- Fix: ask the workspace's owner to press **Publish changes**, or to give you the **May publish and delete** tick. See [People and roles](/workspace/people-and-roles).

**4. Your own wording is in the way.** A greeting you wrote yourself is never changed by other settings. If you typed your hours or business name into it instead of using the short codes, it keeps the old words.

- Check: rows on the **What it says** card with the **Your wording** tag. The row shows exactly what callers hear.
- Fix: use `{hours}`, `{firm}` and `{persona}` in your wording, or empty the box to go back to the standard wording. See [What it says](/receptionist/what-it-says).

**5. The change is to something the receptionist does not use.** **What they do** on a person is a note for you only. To send a kind of call to someone, put them in a team with a **What it covers** description. See [People and teams](/receptionist/people-and-teams).

**6. The change was not saved.** See the next section.

## My changes are not saving

**1. The connection dropped.** The line at the top right says **Not saved. Trying again…**. It keeps trying while the page is open.

- Fix: check your internet connection and keep the page open until it says **Saved**.

**2. The save was refused.** The line says **Not saved.** and a message says why.

- Fix: do what the message says. Your changes stay on the page and are saved again when you next change something.

**3. Someone else changed the receptionist while you had it open.** The line is replaced by, for example, "sam@harbourplumbing.com.au changed this receptionist while you had it open." Nothing more saves until you choose.

- Fix: press **Load their version** to see their changes (your unsaved ones are dropped), or **Keep mine** to save yours over theirs. See [The receptionist page](/receptionist/the-receptionist-page#when-two-people-change-the-same-receptionist).

**4. The card is locked for you.** If you see "Your provider has locked this." beside a card's title, its rows cannot be opened. Ask the workspace's owner. See [Access and locks](/workspace/access-and-locks).

## I cannot go live

When **Go live** is refused, a message box above the Go live bar says why. Nothing reaches callers when it is refused.

**1. There are things to fix.** The bar says, for example, "2 things to fix before the receptionist can go live." and lists them.

- Fix: press each one to go to where it is fixed. See [Things to fix](/receptionist/things-to-fix).

**2. Rows filled in from your website have not been checked.** The bar lists, for example, "Check the 6 things we filled in from your website".

- Fix: check each row with a **From your website** tag, then press **These look right**. See [Create a receptionist](/receptionist/create#check-what-was-filled-in).

**3. The workspace has no payment method.** The message says a payment method is needed before a receptionist can go live.

- Fix: the workspace's owner opens **Billing**, presses **Manage billing** and adds one. Then press **Go live** again. See [Billing](/billing/overview).

**4. The knowledge base is not ready.** The message says the knowledge base has not finished being read, or that it no longer exists.

- Fix: wait until every item says **Ready**, remove any that failed, or choose another knowledge base or **None**. See [What it knows](/receptionist/what-it-knows).

**5. Your account is not allowed to make changes live.** The message says "Your changes are saved, but your account is not allowed to make them live. Ask someone who can go live to press Go live." If you have the **Customer** role without the right, you see that sentence in place of the button.

- Fix: ask the workspace's owner. See [People and roles](/workspace/people-and-roles).

**6. The phone number is used by another receptionist.**

- Fix: choose a spare number under **Phone number** on the **Voice and number** card, then press **Go live** again.

**7. Someone else is going live with it at the same moment.** The message says "this receptionist is being changed by someone else right now; try again in a moment".

- Fix: wait a moment and try again.

**8. Anything else.** For a message that says to tell support, or a list starting **Final check:** that you do not recognise, contact support and include the message. Every message is listed in [Go live](/receptionist/go-live#what-can-stop-it).

## A call is missing from the Calls page

**1. The dates do not cover it.** The list starts with the last 7 days.

- Check: the **From** and **To** dates at the top. Dates and times are in the receptionist's timezone, so a call late at night may fall on the next or previous day.
- Fix: widen the dates, up to a year at a time.

**2. It is on another page of the list.** The list shows 20 calls at a time, newest first.

- Fix: click **Older** at the bottom of the list.

**3. The wrong receptionist is chosen.** Each receptionist has its own calls.

- Check: the **Receptionist** list at the top of the page.
- Fix: choose the receptionist whose number was rung.

**4. The receptionist was not live when the call came in.** Only calls the receptionist answered are shown. A receptionist that has never gone live shows "No calls yet. Calls appear here once your receptionist is live." Calls made while it was stopped after its test minutes ran out were not answered by it.

**5. You cannot see that receptionist.** Someone with the **Customer** role sees only the calls of their own receptionists. See [Access and locks](/workspace/access-and-locks).

If the page says "Call history is not available right now. Try again in a few minutes.", wait and reload the page. See [Call history](/calls/call-history).

## I cannot see Billing, a button or a receptionist

What you see depends on your role in the workspace: **Owner**, **Staff** or **Customer**. See [People and roles](/workspace/people-and-roles).

| What is missing | Why | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Billing** or **Settings** in the side menu | Only an Owner sees them. If you open their address, you see "There is nothing at this address." | Ask an Owner of the workspace. |
| **Calls** in the side menu | Call history has not been set up for your workspace. | Contact support. |
| **Workspaces** in the side menu | It shows only when you belong to more than one workspace. | Nothing to do. See [Several workspaces](/workspace/several-workspaces). |
| A receptionist in the list | A filter is on (**Live** or **Needs attention**), the search box has words in it, or it is deleted and hidden. Someone with the Customer role sees only the receptionists ticked for them. You may also be in another workspace. | Choose **All** and clear the search. Click **show** under the list for deleted ones. Ask the Owner to tick the receptionist for you. |
| **New receptionist** | The Customer role cannot add receptionists. | Ask the Owner. |
| **Go live** | A Customer without **May publish and delete** sees a sentence in its place. | Ask the Owner to publish, or to give you the tick. |
| **Get a new number** | Only an Owner can get a new number. | Ask the Owner. |
| A way to change the phone number | The Customer role sees the number but cannot change it. | Ask the Owner or Staff. |
| A way to choose a knowledge base | The Customer role cannot choose or make one. | Ask the Owner or Staff. See [What it knows](/receptionist/what-it-knows). |
| **Import from a CSV file** | It is only on the **People and teams** page, not on the card, and not shown when the card is locked. | Press **Manage people and teams**. See [Import people from a file](/receptionist/import-people). |
| The pencil on a row, or rows will not open | The card is locked: it says "Your provider has locked this." | Ask the Owner. See [Access and locks](/workspace/access-and-locks). |

## I cannot sign in

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| "These credentials do not match our records." | Check the email address and password, or use **Forgot your password?**. |
| "Too many sign-in attempts. Wait a while and try again, or use “Forgot your password?”." | Wait a while, or reset your password. |
| "No access" | Your address is not in any workspace. Ask the workspace's owner to add you. |
| "There is no account for (your address) yet." after **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Microsoft** | Google or Microsoft cannot make a new account. Ask the owner to add you. |
| "This link no longer works" | The invitation link was used, or is more than 7 days old. Ask for it to be sent again. |
| "Page expired" | Reload the page and sign in again. |
| "That code is not right. Try again." | Type the code your authenticator app shows now. |
| You do not have your phone | Click **Use a recovery code** and type one of your saved codes. |
| You have lost both your phone and your recovery codes | Contact support and ask for two-step sign-in to be reset. |
| An email titled **Wrong sign-in codes** | Signing in is locked for a while. Reset your password with **Forgot your password?**. That also lifts the lock. |

You are signed out after two hours without using the page, unless you ticked **Keep me signed in on this computer**. Changing your password, or someone using **Sign out everywhere**, also signs you out. See [Sign in](/get-started/sign-in) and [Two-step sign-in](/account/two-step-sign-in).

## An email from My Receptionist did not arrive

This is about sign-up, invitation and password emails, not callers' messages. For those, see [A message did not arrive](#a-message-did-not-arrive).

**1. It is in your junk or spam folder.** Look there, and wait a few minutes.

**2. The address was typed wrongly.** Sign up again, or ask for the invitation or reset link again, with the right address.

**3. Too many were asked for.** At most 3 sign-up emails and 3 password reset emails a day go to one address. The page still says the email was sent. Wait until the next day.

**4. The link was replaced.** Only the newest invitation or sign-up link works. Use the latest email.

See [Sign up](/get-started/sign-up) and [Sign in](/get-started/sign-in).

## Still stuck

If you have tried everything here, contact support. Say which receptionist, what you expected, what happened, and, for a call, the date and time from the **Calls** page.
