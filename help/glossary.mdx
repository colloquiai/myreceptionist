---
title: "Glossary"
description: "The words My Receptionist uses, in plain language, with a link to the page that explains each one."
---

The words you meet on My Receptionist's pages, in alphabetical order. Each one links to the page that explains it in full. Where two words are easy to mix up, the entry says so.

## A

**Activity.** The page that records who did what in your workspace, and when: changes to receptionists, people added, settings changed, recordings played. Only an Owner can see it. See [Activity](/workspace/activity).

**Authenticator app.** An app on your phone that shows 6-digit codes that keep changing. You need one for two-step sign-in. See [Two-step sign-in](/account/two-step-sign-in).

**Available.** A person's own hours on the **People and teams** card, for example 08:00 to 16:00. Calls are put through to them only between those times. Not the same as **Office hours**, which are when the whole business is open. See [People and teams](/receptionist/people-and-teams).

## B

**Billing.** The page where an Owner chooses plans, sees minutes used, keeps the payment method and opens invoices. See [Billing](/billing/overview).

**Business name.** On a receptionist's **Your business** card, the name callers hear, for example "Thank you for calling Harbour Plumbing". The business name you type when you sign up is different: it names your workspace. See [Your business](/receptionist/your-business).

## C

**Call recording.** The choice on the **Voice and number** card between **Record calls** and **Do not record calls**. Recordings are played on the **Calls** page. See [Call recording](/on-a-call/call-recording).

**Caller.** Someone who rings your receptionist's number.

**Calls.** The page that lists the calls a receptionist answered, with the conversation, what the receptionist did and the recording. It is shown only where call history is set up for your workspace. See [Call history](/calls/call-history).

**Card.** One of the six boxes on a receptionist's page: **Your business**, **Voice and number**, **People and teams**, **Opening hours**, **What it says** and **What it knows**. See [The receptionist page](/receptionist/the-receptionist-page).

**Checking… and Not known.** Tags beside a receptionist's name while the page finds out whether it is live (**Checking…**), or when it could not find out (**Not known**). See [Go live](/receptionist/go-live#the-tags-by-the-name).

**Confirm it is you.** A page that asks for your password again before an important change to how you sign in. After you confirm, you are not asked again for 15 minutes. See [Confirm it is you](/account/confirm-it-is-you).

**Contact.** The word some messages and the import file use for a person on the **People and teams** card. "Contacts in it" means the people in a team. See [Things to fix](/receptionist/things-to-fix).

**CSV file.** A spreadsheet saved in a simple format, with a comma between each box. You can add many people at once from one. See [Import people from a file](/receptionist/import-people).

**Customer.** One of the three roles. A person with the Customer role sees only the receptionists ticked for them, cannot add or delete receptionists, and cannot change the parts that are locked. It is meant for a business whose receptionist someone else looks after. See [People and roles](/workspace/people-and-roles).

## D

**Dashboard voice.** What the **Voice** row shows when no voice has been picked: the receptionist uses the standard voice. See [Voice and number](/receptionist/voice-and-number).

**Deleted.** A tag on a receptionist that was deleted outside the list. It no longer answers calls. Pressing **Go live** makes it again. See [Go live](/receptionist/go-live#the-tags-by-the-name).

**Department.** The word some messages and the import file use for a team. See [Things to fix](/receptionist/things-to-fix).

**During office hours.** The row on the **Opening hours** card that decides what happens to calls while you are open: **The receptionist answers the call**, or **Put every call straight through**. See [Opening hours](/receptionist/opening-hours).

## E

**Edit panel.** The panel that opens on the right when you click a row. Your changes in it save by themselves. Press **Done** to close it. See [The receptionist page](/receptionist/the-receptionist-page).

## F

**From your website.** A tag on a row that was filled in from your website and still needs checking. Press **These look right** once you have checked it. The receptionist cannot go live while any row has the tag. See [Create a receptionist](/receptionist/create#check-what-was-filled-in).

## G

**Go live.** The button that makes a receptionist answer calls with the version on its page. On a receptionist that is already live, the same button reads **Publish changes**. See [Go live](/receptionist/go-live).

**Go live bar.** The strip above the cards on a receptionist's page. It says what is still to fix, or whether it is ready, and holds the **Go live** or **Publish changes** button. See [Go live](/receptionist/go-live#the-go-live-bar).

**Goodbye.** The last line of every call the receptionist ends. With the standard wording it is "Thank you for calling, Goodbye". See [What it says](/receptionist/what-it-says).

**Greeting.** The first thing a caller hears. There are three: one for when the office is open, one for when it is closed, and one for a public holiday. See [What it says](/receptionist/what-it-says).

## I

**Introduce the caller first.** A choice under **When a call is put through** for a person. The receptionist speaks to them first, says who is calling, then connects the caller. A person's row on the card then says "introduced first". The other choice is **Put straight through**. See [Putting calls through](/on-a-call/putting-calls-through#introduced-first-or-straight-through).

**Invitation.** The email someone gets when an Owner adds them on the **People** page. Its link works once, for 7 days. Until they sign in, the **People** page shows **Invited, not signed in yet**. See [People and roles](/workspace/people-and-roles).

## K

**Keep up to date.** A tick when you add a website to a knowledge base. Its pages are then read again regularly, so changes to your website are picked up. See [What it knows](/receptionist/what-it-knows).

**Knowledge base.** The collection of website pages and files the receptionist answers callers' questions from. You choose it on the **What it knows** card. One knowledge base can be shared by several receptionists. See [What it knows](/receptionist/what-it-knows).

## L

**Live.** The tag on a receptionist that is answering calls. Callers hear the version that last went live, not changes saved since. See [Go live](/receptionist/go-live).

**Lock.** An Owner can lock parts of a receptionist so that people with the Customer role can read them but not change them. A locked card says "Your provider has locked this." Locks never apply to Owner or Staff. See [Access and locks](/workspace/access-and-locks).

## M

**Main contact.** The one person who takes calls that are not clearly for anyone, and messages for nobody in particular. Emailed messages to other people are copied to them. Every receptionist needs exactly one, with an email address. It is often a shared line such as Reception. See [People and teams](/receptionist/people-and-teams#the-main-contact).

**Main contact only.** A choice under **Send their messages by** for a person: their messages go to the main contact, not to them. A new person starts with this choice. See [Taking messages](/on-a-call/taking-messages).

**Manage billing.** The button on the **Billing** page that opens the separate payments page, where the card, billing email and address are changed and plans are cancelled. See [Billing](/billing/overview).

**May publish and delete.** A tick for a Staff or Customer person. With it they can make changes live. Staff with it can also delete a receptionist that has gone live. A Customer can never delete. An Owner always has this right. See [People and roles](/workspace/people-and-roles).

**Minutes.** Call time used by a receptionist on a plan. Each plan includes a number of minutes a month, and the **Billing** page shows how many are used and when the count starts again. Part of a minute counts as a whole minute. See [Plans and minutes](/billing/plans-and-minutes).

## N

**Name in your list.** What a receptionist is called on your **Receptionists** page. Callers never hear it. See [Your business](/receptionist/your-business).

**Needs attention.** A filter. On the **Receptionists** page it shows the receptionists that are not live. On the **People and teams** page it shows the people with something to fix or worth a look. See [Find your way around](/get-started/find-your-way).

**Not live.** The tag on a receptionist that has never gone live. It does not answer calls. See [Go live](/receptionist/go-live#the-tags-by-the-name).

**Not live yet.** The tag on a receptionist that was prepared for going live but not made live, for example because going live stopped part way. Press **Go live** again. See [Go live](/receptionist/go-live#the-tags-by-the-name).

## O

**Office hours.** When your business is open, set on the **Opening hours** card. Calls are put through only during these hours. Outside them, callers hear the closed greeting. Not the same as a person's **Available** times. See [Opening hours](/receptionist/opening-hours).

**Owner.** The role that can do everything in a workspace, including **Billing**, **People**, **Activity** and **Settings**. Whoever signs up is the Owner of the new workspace. A workspace can have more than one. See [People and roles](/workspace/people-and-roles).

## P

**Payment method.** The card kept on the payments page for your workspace. A receptionist cannot go live, and an Owner cannot get a new number, until there is one. See [Billing](/billing/overview).

**People.** In the side menu, the page listing the people who can sign in to your workspace, with their roles. Only an Owner sees it. Not the same as **People and teams**. See [People and roles](/workspace/people-and-roles).

**People and teams.** On a receptionist's page, the card listing the people and teams callers can be put through to, with their numbers, hours and how messages reach them. It also has a page of its own, opened with **Manage people and teams**. Not the same as **People** in the side menu. See [People and teams](/receptionist/people-and-teams).

**Person.** On the **People and teams** card, someone callers can ask for, or a shared line such as Reception. Each has a name, numbers, an email, hours and choices for calls and messages. See [People and teams](/receptionist/people-and-teams).

**Plan.** What keeps a receptionist answering calls once it is paid for. Each receptionist has its own plan, chosen on the **Billing** page after it has gone live. The Billing page shows the plans and prices. See [Plans and minutes](/billing/plans-and-minutes).

**Provider.** In the message "Your provider has locked this.", whoever looks after your receptionist: the Owner or Staff of the workspace. See [Access and locks](/workspace/access-and-locks).

**Public holiday.** A date in the **Public holidays** list on the **Opening hours** card. On these dates callers hear the public holiday greeting and calls are not put through. Only the dates you add count. See [Closed and public holidays](/on-a-call/closed-and-public-holidays).

**Publish changes.** The **Go live** button on a receptionist that is already live. It sends the changes you have saved to callers. Until you press it, callers hear the older version. See [Go live](/receptionist/go-live).

**Put every call straight through.** A choice under **During office hours**. Every call during office hours goes to one person you choose, with nothing asked. Not the same as **Put straight through**, which is set on each person. See [Putting calls through](/on-a-call/putting-calls-through#putting-every-call-straight-through).

**Put straight through.** A choice under **When a call is put through** for a person: the caller is connected with no introduction. The other choice is **Introduce the caller first**. See [Putting calls through](/on-a-call/putting-calls-through#introduced-first-or-straight-through).

## R

**Receptionist.** One phone answerer, with its own name, voice, phone number, hours, people and wording. A workspace can have several. See [Create a receptionist](/receptionist/create).

**Receptionist's name.** The name it gives callers, for example "This is Ava". A new receptionist starts as Ava. Not the same as **Name in your list**. See [Your business](/receptionist/your-business).

**Recovery codes.** Codes you are given once when you switch on two-step sign-in. Each one lets you sign in once without your phone. Keep them somewhere safe. See [Two-step sign-in](/account/two-step-sign-in).

**Row.** One line on a card, such as **Business name** or a person's name. Click it to open the edit panel. See [The receptionist page](/receptionist/the-receptionist-page).

## S

**Saved.** The line at the top right of a receptionist's page when your changes are kept. Saved is not the same as live: callers hear saved changes only after **Go live** or **Publish changes**. **Not saved.** means the save did not go through. See [The receptionist page](/receptionist/the-receptionist-page#saving-happens-by-itself).

**Short codes.** `{firm}`, `{persona}` and `{hours}`. Used in your own wording, they are replaced with the business name, the receptionist's name and your hours, so your greetings stay right when those change. See [What it says](/receptionist/what-it-says).

**Short name.** A name made of lowercase letters, digits and hyphens that tells receptionists apart. You type it only if two receptionists have the same business name. The **Activity** page names receptionists by it. See [Create a receptionist](/receptionist/create).

**Sign out everywhere.** A button on your account page that signs you out on every computer and phone at once. See [Your password and sign-in methods](/account/password#signing-out-everywhere).

**Staff.** The role that can work on every receptionist, including going live with the **May publish and delete** tick, but cannot see **Billing**, **People**, **Activity** or **Settings**. See [People and roles](/workspace/people-and-roles).

**Standard wording.** What the receptionist says when you leave a greeting or the goodbye empty. It uses your business name, the receptionist's name and your hours, and it follows your choices on the **Opening hours** card. See [What it says](/receptionist/what-it-says).

**Stopped taking calls.** A red tag on the **Billing** page on a receptionist whose test minutes are used up. It does not answer calls until it is on a plan. Its number and set-up are kept. Only the Billing page shows this tag. See [Test minutes](/billing/test-minutes).

## T

**Team.** On the **People and teams** card, a group callers can ask for by name, such as Accounts, or reach by saying what they need. It has a name, **What it covers**, and the people in it. See [People and teams](/receptionist/people-and-teams#add-a-team).

**Template.** A choice in the **New receptionist** box: the kind of receptionist it starts from, which sets how a call goes. Leave the first choice unless you were told to pick another. See [Create a receptionist](/receptionist/create).

**Test minutes.** Free minutes a receptionist may have before it is on a plan. They are for the life of the receptionist and do not start again each month. When they are used up it stops answering until you choose a plan. A receptionist using them has a **Trying it out** tag on the Billing page. See [Test minutes](/billing/test-minutes).

**Things to fix.** Problems the page finds that stop a receptionist going live, shown in red on the row and listed in the Go live bar. Problems in amber are only **worth a look** and do not stop it. See [Things to fix](/receptionist/things-to-fix).

**Timezone.** Your business's local time, set on the **Your business** card. Office hours, public holidays, each person's **Available** times and the times on the **Calls** page all go by it. See [Your business](/receptionist/your-business).

**Trying it out.** A blue tag on the **Billing** page on a receptionist that is using its test minutes. See [Test minutes](/billing/test-minutes).

**Two-step sign-in.** Signing in with your password and then a code from an authenticator app on your phone, so someone with only your password cannot get in. See [Two-step sign-in](/account/two-step-sign-in).

## V

**Voice.** How the receptionist sounds, chosen on the **Voice and number** card. Press **▶ Play sample** to hear one. See [Voice and number](/receptionist/voice-and-number).

## W

**What it covers.** A team's description, for example "buying and selling property". It tells the receptionist which calls belong to the team. See [People and teams](/receptionist/people-and-teams#add-a-team).

**What they do.** A note on a person's role, for you. The receptionist does not use it. To send a kind of call to someone, put them in a team instead. See [People and teams](/receptionist/people-and-teams).

**Words to listen for.** A list of names and unusual words, such as staff names and place names, that helps the receptionist hear them correctly. It is not spoken. It cannot be empty. See [What it says](/receptionist/what-it-says#words-to-listen-for).

**Workspace.** Your business's account. Your receptionists, call history, billing and the people who can sign in all belong to it. One email address can belong to several. See [Several workspaces](/workspace/several-workspaces).

**Worth a look.** A problem shown in amber. It does not stop the receptionist going live, but a caller may run into it. See [Things to fix](/receptionist/things-to-fix).

## Y

**Your wording.** A tag on a line on the **What it says** card that you wrote yourself instead of the standard wording. Your wording is never changed by other settings. Empty the box to go back to the standard wording. See [What it says](/receptionist/what-it-says).
